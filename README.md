# BIOCHEM-2000
This course establishes the strategic, technical, and operational framework for the VirusTC and Pacific Health [pH] Medical Center distributed telehealth network.

Overview of the Telehealth Framework

-   **High-Containment Architecture:** Maps out USAMRIID-aligned isolation procedures, utilizing hardware provisioning (medical laptops, cameras, and balanced lighting kits) to conduct safe virtual examinations. It formalizes the cancellation of the physical hospital at Yesler Towers in favor of training-only infrastructure to safeguard urban municipal sewer systems.
-   **Diagnostic Protocol:** Details systematic visual assessment guidelines and structured clinical interviewing routines tailored for severe pathological conditions.
-   **Rushed Pharmaceutical Delivery:** Establishes workflows for rapid logistics dispatch immediately following digital prescription sign-offs.
-   **Geographic Infrastructure Mapping:** Outlines the dual-layer network dividing remote triage from physical interventions across your **23-state clinic footprint** and primary Seattle anchors:
    -   **University of Washington's Harborview Medical Center**
    -   **UW Medical Center Campus**
    -   **UW Department of Medicine (Ballard)**
